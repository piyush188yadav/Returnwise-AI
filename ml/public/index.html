How ReturnWise AI Works

From order checkout to proactive merchant intervention in three simple steps.

1

Ingest Order Signals

Captures item category, sizing, price, expected shipping days, customer return history, star rating, and payment method.

2

ML Pipeline Evaluation

Python microservice applies the trained scikit-learn pipeline, evaluating feature weights to calculate a calibrated risk score (0-100).

3

Proactive Interventions

Generates the top three contributing factors and pairs them with two targeted seller actions before the parcel leaves the fulfillment center.
